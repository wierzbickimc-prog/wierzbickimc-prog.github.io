Privacy policy

Updated November 10, 2026. This policy describes the current Our Days household desktop calendar and this informational website.

Google Calendar access

With your consent, Our Days requests the Google Calendar read-only permission to list calendars, let you choose which calendars to display, and retrieve their events. It uses account and calendar identifiers, calendar labels, event identifiers, titles, start and end times, all-day status, and locations to organize and display your schedule. The application does not display event descriptions.

Our Days does not request Gmail access or permission to create, modify, or delete Google Calendar events. Sign-in occurs through Google; Our Days does not collect your Google password.

Purpose and visibility

Calendar data is used to show the selected calendars on your household display and retain a saved view during connection outages. Anyone who can see that display may see the selected events. Choose calendars accordingly.

Storage and retention

Google OAuth credentials, refresh tokens, account and calendar configuration, and cached event snapshots are stored on the computer running Our Days. The application writes its private credential and cache files with owner-only file permissions. These permissions do not provide encryption. Saved snapshots may remain until the household administrator deletes them; changing the selected calendars or revoking Google access does not automatically erase old local snapshots.

Sharing and other services

The desktop application communicates with Google to authorize and retrieve calendars. Its current implementation does not upload calendar events to this informational website, advertising services, or analytics services. It does not sell calendar data or use it for advertising or training AI models.

If configured, the app also contacts your Immich server for album photos, Open-Meteo for weather, and your local IKEA TRÅDFRI gateway for light controls. Calendar events are not included in those requests. Those services process their own connection requests under their respective policies. Photo previews and optional service credentials are stored locally.

Disconnecting and deleting data

You can revoke Our Days’ access in your Google Account’s third-party connections settings. To remove locally stored information, ask the household administrator to stop the application and delete the relevant Google token files, calendar configuration, and calendar cache from the application’s private storage. Revoking access alone does not delete downloaded data.

This website

These static pages do not include analytics scripts, advertising, tracking cookies, or forms collecting calendar data. The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses and browser details, to deliver and secure the website. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages; see GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of website requests.

Contact

Contact the administrator of your household display or the user-support email shown on Our Days’ Google consent screen with access, deletion, or privacy questions.
