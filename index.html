A calendar for home

Our days,
together.

Our Days is a native desktop application for a household calendar display. It brings selected Google calendars into a shared view, alongside family photos and local weather.

Your calendars, at a glance

See the current and next weeks, browse months, and open a day for event details. Our Days asks for read-only Google Calendar access. It does not create, edit, or delete calendar events, and does not request Gmail access.

Made for a private household

The application runs on a household computer. Google authorization credentials and saved calendar snapshots are stored on that computer. This informational website does not display calendars or provide a Google sign-in form.

Photos and everyday details

Optional features include photos from a local folder or a configured Immich album, weather from Open-Meteo, and controls for a configured IKEA TRÅDFRI gateway.

Support

For help, contact the person who installed your household display. Google authorization questions can also be directed to the support email shown on the app’s Google consent screen.
